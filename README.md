# Pokemon DB

The data contains information about Pokémon, their abilities, moves, evolution, types,
forms, and other related elements like the moves themselves and items. The data originated
from the main series Pokémon games from generations 1-8 inclusive. Side games are not
included. Generation 9 wasn’t released until the due date for part 2, and therefore was not
included. This data was provided by Pokémon Essentials, a fan-made tool used for making
Pokémon fan games. This data belongs to Nintendo and the Pokémon Company and they do
not endorse its use here. Supplementary data was sourced from Bulbapedia, a Pokémon wiki,
and its data is similarly owned by Nintendo and the Pokémon Company.
